✦ Who am I

I care about whether software actually holds up once it's in someone's hands, not just whether it runs, but whether it does what people were promised and expected it to do. That's the lens I bring to my work: is this stable enough to ship, and will it genuinely serve the people using it? I've built that instinct over years of professional experience, well beyond my time in QA. The quality of a piece of software isn't abstract to me, it shows up in someone's day, in whether their work runs smoothly or gets derailed by something that shouldn't have shipped.

The Journey

My path here wasn't a straight line into QA. I started in banking, then moved through customer support, business operations, and data analysis, each role sharpening a different piece of what I now bring to testing: precision under pressure, root-cause thinking, defect documentation, and data-driven judgment. An MSc in Business Analytics & Big Data and ISTQB certification turned that experience into a formal QA skillset, and I've carried the same instinct across every stop: I don't just check boxes, I care about what "quality" actually means to the people on the other end of the product.
